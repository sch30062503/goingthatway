Terms of use

The plain-English rules for using Going That Way, whether you're sending something or driving. By creating an account or posting a job, you agree to them.

1. What Going That Way is

Going That Way is a website that connects people who need something moved between towns on State Highway 1, from Christchurch to Dunedin, with people who are already driving that way. We check everyone's ID, set the price, take payment, and pay the driver once the job is done.

When a driver takes your job, the agreement to carry your item is between you and that driver. Going That Way arranges it and handles the money, but we don't take possession of your item and we aren't the carrier. Drivers are independent people using their own vehicles. They aren't our employees, agents or contractors.

This is a trial service, so some things will change as we learn.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or over, live in New Zealand, and give your real name, mobile number and address. One account per person.
  • Everyone is ID-checked once with a photo ID and a selfie. Drivers also need a full or restricted driver licence (not a learner licence) and a vehicle with a current WoF and rego.
  • Keep your password to yourself, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account. You're responsible for what's done using it.
  • A business can use one account for its staff. The business is responsible for what its staff do on it.
  • You can close your account at any time by asking us. Jobs already under way still need to be finished or cancelled under these terms.

3. Posting a job: what you promise

  • The item is yours (or you have the owner's permission), it's legal to carry, and it isn't on our list of things we won't carry.
  • You've described it honestly, including its size, its condition and whether it's a two-person lift.
  • The pickup and delivery details are right, and someone will be there when you said, or it will be left where you said.
  • Anything fragile is packed so it can travel safely in a car, ute or trailer.
  • If you give us someone else's details, such as a seller's name and number, they know a Going That Way driver will contact them to arrange collection.
  • Pick-up-only purchases: you've bought it, and the seller is expecting it to be collected.
  • Click and collect: it's paid for and ready, and the order number and name are right. If the store needs you to name who's collecting, add your driver's name once they take the job.
  • Two-person lifts: someone will help the driver load it and someone will help unload it. Drivers usually travel alone.
  • Left out for collection: if you ask a driver to collect from, or leave it at, a place where nobody's there, it's at your risk while it's unattended.

4. Things we won't carry

  • Dangerous goods: fuel, gas bottles, chemicals, explosives, ammunition, or anything flammable, corrosive or toxic
  • Firearms and other weapons
  • Drugs, prescription medicines, alcohol, tobacco and vaping products
  • Cash, cards, jewellery, precious metals and other valuables
  • Live animals, animal parts and human remains
  • Food or anything else that must be kept cold
  • Stolen, counterfeit or illegal goods, or anything restricted by biosecurity rules
  • Anything worth more than $500 during the trial

A driver can look at an item before taking it, and can refuse anything sealed, suspicious, unsafe or not as described.

5. Price, payment and fees

  • You see one all-in price before you post, including GST where it applies. You pay when you post, and the job goes live once we've received the payment.
  • Your payment goes into Going That Way's business bank account. We pay the driver once the job is done. We don't hold it on trust or as an escrow service.
  • Our fee is 15% (at least $3). Everything else goes to the driver, including any urgency bonus and the "check it first" fee.
  • Prices and fees may change, but the price shown when you post is the price you pay for that job.
  • Drivers are paid weekly by bank transfer, to the account they add under My account, for jobs they've completed.
  • Arrange and pay for jobs only through Going That Way. Arranging a job you found here directly with the other person, to avoid the fee, isn't allowed.

6. Cancellations, no-shows and refunds

  • Before a driver takes the job: cancel in the app for a full refund.
  • No driver by your deliver-by day, or by the end of the day for an urgent job: full refund.
  • After a driver has taken it, but before pickup: contact us. If the driver hasn't set off, you'll usually get a full refund. If they have, the driver may be paid for their trip.
  • No-show: if the driver arrives within the time you said and, after waiting 15 minutes and trying to contact you, can't collect it (nobody's there, it isn't ready, it isn't as described, there's no help with a two-person lift, or a store won't release a click and collect order), the driver is paid for their trip and we refund the rest.
  • "Check it first" and you say no: it isn't collected, the driver is paid for their trip, and we refund the rest.
  • After pickup: once an item has been collected, the job can only be cancelled by agreement with us. If it has to come back, the driver returns it to where it was collected.
  • If the driver cancels: the job goes back on the board for another driver. If nobody takes it in time, you get a full refund.
  • Late for a set arrival time or same-day express: you pay the normal rate, and we refund the difference.
  • Refunds go back to the account you paid from, usually within 2 working days.

7. Collection and delivery

  • The driver texts before setting off, and takes a photo at pickup and at drop-off. The drop-off photo is our record that the item was delivered.
  • A driver can refuse or cancel a job if the item isn't as described, is bigger or heavier than listed, isn't safe to carry, or there's no help with a two-person lift.
  • Weather, road closures, crashes and other things outside anyone's control can cause delays.

8. Loss or damage

During the trial, items are not insured and are carried at owner's risk under the Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017. That means neither the driver nor Going That Way is liable for loss of or damage to an item, unless it's caused intentionally. You agree to this when you post a job. Please only send things you could afford to lose, worth under $500, and pack anything fragile.

If something arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery, with photos, using Report a problem on the job. Report anything else within 30 days. We'll look into every report and help sort it out with the driver.

Going That Way isn't liable for indirect losses, such as lost income or the cost of a missed opportunity. Where we are liable to you, our total liability for a job is limited to the greater of the fee we charged for that job or $50, except where the law doesn't allow that limit.

Nothing in these terms takes away any rights you have under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986, where those Acts apply.

9. Problems and disputes

If something goes wrong, use Report a problem on the job. We'll look at the photos and messages, talk to both of you, and suggest a fair outcome, such as a full or part refund. Please work with us in good faith. If a dispute between a sender and a driver still can't be resolved, either of you can take it further, for example to the Disputes Tribunal.

10. For drivers

  • You're an independent driver, not an employee. You choose whether to post a trip and which jobs to take. You're never obliged to take a job, there's no minimum, and you use your own vehicle at your own cost.
  • Only take jobs you can do safely and legally. Do them yourself, with reasonable care, and secure every load properly. Don't overload your vehicle or trailer.
  • Keep your licence, WoF and rego current, and tell us if anything changes. You can't take jobs while your WoF or rego has expired.
  • Check that your vehicle insurance covers carrying other people's goods for payment. Many policies don't by default.
  • What you earn is your income. You're responsible for your own tax, and for GST if you're registered.
  • Treat people's addresses, phone numbers and belongings with care. Use them only for the job.

11. Photos, ratings and messages

Photos and reports you add stay yours, but you let us use them to run the service and sort out problems. Ratings and notes must be honest and fair. We can remove anything abusive, untrue or unrelated to a job.

12. Behaviour and suspension

Be honest, polite and safe. We can suspend or close the account of anyone who breaks these terms, misleads or threatens another member, cancels unfairly or repeatedly, or uses Going That Way to move anything illegal. Where the law requires it, we'll help the police.

13. Things outside our control

We're not responsible for delays or failures caused by events outside our reasonable control, such as severe weather, road closures, natural disasters, or outages at the services we rely on.

14. Changes, notices and the law

  • We may update these terms. We'll show the date they changed at the top, and tell you by email about important changes. Jobs already posted stay under the terms you agreed to.
  • We'll contact you by email, text or a notice in the app, and that counts as writing.
  • If any part of these terms can't be enforced, the rest still applies.
  • These terms are the whole agreement between us about using Going That Way. New Zealand law applies.

15. Contact

To get in touch, use "Report a problem" on your job in the app.
