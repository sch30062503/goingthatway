Terms of use

The plain-English rules for using Going That Way, whether you're sending something or driving.

1. What Going That Way is

Going That Way connects people who need something carried between towns on State Highway 1, from Christchurch to Dunedin, with people who are already driving that way. We run the website, check everyone's ID, take payment, and pay the driver once the job is done. The drivers are independent people using their own vehicles. They aren't our employees.

This is a trial service, so we're still learning and some things will change.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or over and give your real name, mobile number and address.
  • Everyone is ID-checked once with a photo ID and a selfie. Drivers also need a full or restricted driver licence (not a learner licence) and a vehicle with a current WoF and rego.
  • Keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for what's done using your account.
  • We can suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or that we think is being misused.

3. Posting a job

  • Describe the item honestly, including its size and whether it's a two-person lift.
  • For a pick-up-only purchase, you must have bought it, and the seller must be expecting it to be collected.
  • For a click and collect order, it must be paid for and ready to collect, and you must give the right order number and name. If the store needs you to name who's collecting, add your driver's name once they take the job. If the driver can't collect it because the order isn't ready or they aren't allowed to, it's treated as a no-show.
  • Make sure it's available when and where you said. If it's a two-person lift, someone must help the driver load it and someone must help unload it. Drivers usually travel alone.
  • The deliver-by day is the last day for delivery. The more days you allow, the more likely a driver can take it.

4. Things we won't carry

Dangerous goods (such as fuel, gas bottles, chemicals or explosives), cash or valuables, drugs, firearms or other weapons, live animals, anything illegal, and anything worth more than $500 during the trial. A driver can look at an item before taking it and can refuse anything sealed, suspicious, unsafe or not as described.

5. Price and payment

  • You see one all-in price before you post. You pay when you post, and we hold the money until the item is delivered.
  • Our fee is 15% (at least $3). Everything else goes to the driver, including any urgency bonus and the "check it first" fee.
  • Drivers are paid weekly by bank transfer, to the account they add under My account, for jobs they've completed.

6. Cancellations and refunds

  • Before a driver takes the job: cancel in the app for a full refund.
  • No driver by your deliver-by day, or by the end of the day for an urgent job: full refund.
  • After a driver has taken it: contact us. If the driver hasn't set off, we'll usually refund in full.
  • Not available when the driver arrives at the time you said (a no-show): the driver is paid for their trip, and we refund the rest.
  • "Check it first" and you say no: it isn't collected, the driver is paid for their trip, and we refund the rest.
  • Late for a set arrival time or same-day express: you pay the normal rate, and we refund the difference.

7. Collection and delivery

Drivers text before they set off, and take a photo at pickup and at drop-off as a record. Weather, road closures, crashes and other things outside anyone's control can cause delays.

8. Loss or damage

During the trial, items are not insured and are carried at owner's risk under the Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017. That means neither Going That Way nor the driver is liable for loss of or damage to an item, unless it's caused intentionally. Please only send things you could afford to lose, worth under $500, and pack anything fragile.

If something goes wrong, use Report a problem on the job within 30 days, with photos if you can. We'll look into every report and help sort it out with the driver.

Nothing in these terms takes away any rights you have under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986, where those Acts apply.

9. For drivers

  • Only take jobs you can do safely and legally. Secure every load properly, and don't overload your vehicle or trailer.
  • Keep your licence, WoF and rego current, and tell us if anything changes. You can't take jobs while your WoF or rego has expired.
  • Check that your vehicle insurance covers carrying other people's goods for payment. Many policies don't by default.
  • What you earn is your income. You're responsible for your own tax, and for GST if you're registered.
  • Treat people's addresses, phone numbers and belongings with care. Use them only for the job.

10. Behaviour

Be honest, polite and safe. We'll close the account of anyone who threatens, harasses or misleads another member, or who uses Going That Way to move anything illegal. Where the law requires it, we'll help the police.

11. Changes and the law

We may update these terms, and will show the date they changed at the top. New Zealand law applies.

12. Contact

To get in touch, use "Report a problem" on your job in the app.
